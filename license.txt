Solace is open-source software released under the MIT licence. The full text is in the LICENSE file that comes with the source code.

1. PRIVACY POLICY: Your data stays with you.
Solace stores your therapy data locally on this device only. Nothing is sent to the internet without your action.

By installing and using Solace, you agree that the software may locally store the following on your device:

- Movement data: Your daily range-of-motion readings, stored on this device only.
- Session history: Which games you played, for how long, and at what difficulty.
- Wellbeing check-ins: How you felt after each session (Good / Tired / Pain).
- Progress reports: Only shared when YOU tap 'Download Report' and save or send the PDF.
